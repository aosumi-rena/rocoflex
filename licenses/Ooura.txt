Rocoflex's src/fft.rs is a Rust adaptation of fftsg_h.c from this package,
using single-precision working storage and the original arithmetic ordering.

The double-precision resampling transform in
src/resample44100/ooura_table_f64.rs is adapted from fft4g.c, not fftsg_h.c.
Its separate attribution and upstream readme notice are in Ooura-fft4g.txt.
