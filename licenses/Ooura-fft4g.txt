Takuya Ooura, General Purpose FFT Package, 2006-12-28.
https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~ooura/fft.html

Rocoflex's src/resample44100/ooura_table_f64.rs is a mechanical Rust adaptation
of fft4g.c from this package. It uses double-precision working storage, packed
real forward transforms and unscaled inverse transforms. Arithmetic order and
persistent integer/twiddle workspace follow that implementation. Trigonometric
services are supplied explicitly by the caller.
